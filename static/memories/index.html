Memories

Relive this day, every year.

Memories shows you every photo and video you took on this day in years past, straight from your photo library.

  • Relive this day, every yearToday’s photos and videos from every year gone by, grouped by year in a single scrolling grid.
  • Every memory, full screenSwipe between photos, pinch to zoom, and play videos and Live Photos.
  • See when and where it was takenSwipe up on any photo to see the date, the camera it was taken with and a map of where you were.
  • Share, favourite or delete in one goSelect as many photos as you like and share them, favourite them or tidy them away.
  • Jump to any day with memoriesThe Timeline shows how many photos you have for each day, so you can head straight to the good ones.
  • On your Home ScreenA widget shows a photo from this day in a past year. Tap it to jump straight to that day.
  • A gentle daily reminderGet a notification each day to let you know when you have memories to look back on.
  • Private by designYour photos stay on your device and in iCloud Photos. Memories never uploads, collects or analyses them.
