Privacy Policy

Memories does not collect, store or share any of your personal data.

Your photos and videos are read directly from your photo library on your device. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere by Memories. Any network access to your photos is made by Apple’s own frameworks to fetch them from iCloud Photos.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Memories uses RevenueCat to check the status of your purchases. To do this, RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated identifier and your App Store purchase history. It does not receive your name, your email address or any of your photos.

Memories contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking. This website doesn’t use cookies or analytics either.

If you choose to share crash reports and diagnostics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may share anonymised crash data with us. It contains no personal data.

Use of Memories is subject to Apple’s standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.
